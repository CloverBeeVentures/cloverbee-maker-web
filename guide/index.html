Using Maker

Maker Guide

Set up a business, turn materials into finished stock, and follow that stock through a sale. The labels and workflows below were checked against the current Maker code and a current Android build.

Before Getting Started

Create your account, then choose your business path

Create and confirm your Maker account

  1. After installing Maker, open the app.
  2. In Email, enter an address you can access. If someone invited you, use the exact address that received the invitation.
  3. In Password, enter the password you want to use, then tap Create account.
  4. When Maker says Check your email to confirm the account, open that message and use its confirmation link. The link returns you to Maker.
  5. If the message does not arrive, check junk or spam, then return to Maker and tap Resend confirmation email.
  6. After confirmation, enter the same Email and Password and tap Sign in.

After signing in: Maker opens Set up Maker. Choose one path below—create a new business or join an existing one.

Option A — Start a new business

  1. On Set up Maker, scroll to Create a business.
  2. Enter the public-facing name in Business name.
  3. Tap Create business. Maker creates the business, registers this device, and opens the business workspace.

Option B — Accept an invitation

  1. Open the secure invitation link from the business owner, or copy the invitation code they gave you.
  2. Create or sign in to Maker with the exact email address that received the invitation.
  3. On Set up Maker, find Join a team. If the link did not fill the field, enter the code in Invitation code.
  4. Review the displayed business and role when available, then tap Accept invitation.
  5. If Maker reports that the device is pending approval, ask the business owner to approve it before continuing.
Common mistake: Do not create a new business when you intend to join someone else’s. An invitation belongs to the invited email address; use Use a different account if you signed in with the wrong one.

Getting Started

Know the five main tabs

Maker opens on Home. The navigation bar contains Home, Sell, Orders, Stock, and More.

  1. Open Home and confirm the correct business name appears.
  2. Tap Sync now while connected. Wait for Sync complete.
  3. Open Stock for Products, Recipes, Make, and Inventory.
  4. Open More for business settings, reports, expenses, taxes, plan, and team access.

Next: Review the business settings before adding stock.

Offline work and sync

Maker saves normal work locally and queues it for sync. If sync fails, keep the app installed and retry when connected. Do not clear app data or uninstall while unresolved local operations may exist.

02

Creating your business

Create the workspace your records belong to

  1. On Set up Maker, find Create a business.
  2. Enter the public-facing Business name. Example: Prairie Glow Candle Co.
  3. Tap Create business.
  4. After Maker opens, go to More → Business settings.
  5. Enter the required Business name and Business type, then choose Province or state. Confirm the fiscal-year start and optional contact/address fields.
  6. Scroll to the end and tap Save.

After saving: Maker uses the selected location for currency display and timezone. Changes save locally, then sync. Next: configure taxes.

Common mistake: Invitation codes join an existing business. Do not create a second business when you meant to accept a team invitation.

See Step 0: The current Set up Maker screen is shown above with both the invitation and new-business paths.

03

Business settings and taxes

Add only the tax rules your business uses

  1. Open More → Tax & Export.
  2. Tap Add tax.
  3. Enter the tax name and rate shown on your own records, and choose whether it is enabled and applied by default.
  4. Save the tax rule. When applicable, select the rule on products, sales, orders, receipt items, and extra charges.

After saving: new transactions can snapshot the selected tax rule. Existing completed transaction snapshots are not silently rewritten. Next: add a product.

Common mistake: Do not add a tax merely because it is common in your region. Maker records the settings you provide; it does not determine your legal tax obligations.
04

Adding your first product

Create the thing you sell

  1. Open Stock → Products & variants.
  2. Tap Product (the add button).
  3. Enter Product name. Example: Amber Jar Candle.
  4. Optionally enter Description. Enter the normal selling amount in Base price, such as 24.00.
  5. Select any applicable tax rules. Leave Active on. Use Favourite for products you want to reach quickly.
  6. Tap Save product.

After saving: Maker creates the product and its first sellable variant. Open the product later to add exact variants, SKUs, or variant prices. Next: add every material and packaging item used by one unit.

Common mistake: Put the customer price in Base price; material cost comes from receiving and Recipes. Do not type production cost into the selling-price field.
05

Adding materials and packaging

Track ingredients, components, and containers the same way

  1. Open Stock → Inventory.
  2. Under Materials setup, open Materials.
  3. Tap Add material.
  4. Enter the material name and description. Choose the Standard unit used by the Recipe.
  5. Optionally choose Preferred vendor. Turn on Track best-before / expiry only when it applies.
  6. Leave Active on and tap Save material.
  7. Repeat for packaging. Candle example: Soy Wax in kg, Vanilla Fragrance in ml, Cotton Wick in ea, and Amber Jar + Lid in ea.

After saving: the material can be selected in a Recipe and on a receiving line. Next: build the product Recipe.

Common mistake: Pick the Recipe unit before creating history. Maker blocks a material-unit change after Recipe or inventory history exists.
06

Creating a recipe

Define materials per one finished unit

  1. Open Stock → Recipes.
  2. Tap Recipe.
  3. Choose the exact Finished product variant.
  4. Under Materials per 1 finished unit, choose a material and enter its Per unit quantity.
  5. Tap Add material line for every additional ingredient or package.
  6. Leave Recipe active on and tap Save Recipe.
Prairie Glow example1 Amber Jar Candle = 0.25 kg soy wax + 10 ml fragrance + 1 wick + 1 jar/lid. A batch of 12 therefore previews 3 kg wax, 120 ml fragrance, 12 wicks, and 12 jars/lids.

After saving: the Recipe becomes available on Make. It does not change inventory until a Make run is recorded. Next: receive the materials.

Common mistake: Enter quantities for one finished unit, not for the whole batch. A material may appear only once in a Recipe.
07

Receiving materials and recording production

Bring materials in, then turn them into finished stock

Receive a purchase

  1. Open Stock → Inventory → Add inventory.
  2. Choose Vendor if known and enter the optional Invoice / receipt number.
  3. Tap Add line. Choose the material, quantity, unit cost, and applicable taxes. Repeat for every item.
  4. Add delivery or other amounts under Extra charges when needed.
  5. Review Invoice total, then tap Save receipt and add stock.

After saving: material On Hand and current known cost update; the receipt is available in receiving history.

Record production

  1. Open Stock → Make.
  2. Choose the Finished product variant and enter Quantity to make — 12 in the candle example.
  3. Check Required materials. Resolve any SHORT warning unless you intentionally accept negative material inventory.
  4. Add an optional note and tap Record Make.

After saving: the Recipe inputs are consumed, 12 finished candles are added, and the batch appears in Make history with its preserved material quantities and known costs.

Common mistake: Maker can continue after a shortage confirmation, which makes that material balance negative. Correct the receipt or count first when the shortage is accidental.
08

Making your first sale

Use Sell when the product leaves now

  1. Open Sell and select New sale.
  2. Choose the product and quantity — 2 Amber Jar Candles in the example.
  3. Choose a customer when useful. Confirm the displayed price and tax.
  4. Choose payment status and method. Maker supports manual methods and a Square Point of Sale handoff where configured.
  5. Tap Save.

After saving: the sale is recorded and two finished candles leave On Hand. Starting from 12, the example leaves 10. Material inventory does not change again because it was consumed during the Make run. Next: review the sale in Sales history or use Orders for later delivery.

Common mistake: A product handed to the customer now is a Sale even when unpaid. Use an Order when delivery is later; payment and delivery are separate states.
09

Orders, customers, and inventory

Track promises separately from hand-offs

  • Orders: open Orders, add products, set the promised date, record payments separately, and save. Fulfil only the quantity actually delivered.
  • Customers: open More → Customers to save repeat-customer details and link activity.
  • Inventory: open Stock → Inventory → View inventory to compare On Hand, Reserved, and Available. A saved Order reserves demand; fulfilment removes physical stock.
10

Expenses and reporting

Keep the record while the year is happening

  1. Open More → Expenses & Product Families to record non-inventory spending and manage categories.
  2. Open More → Reports for Sales history, orders, inventory, receiving, counts, and tax records.
  3. Open More → Tax & Export for the Free basic export; Paid adds purpose-specific operational exports and deeper analysis.
11

Paid subscriptions and team members

Upgrade the business, then invite the team

  1. Open More → Plan & upgrade. Choose the available monthly or annual Maker Paid product, or use Restore Purchases.
  2. Wait while Maker securely verifies the store purchase. A store completion alone does not unlock Paid.
  3. Open More → Team & devices.
  4. Under Invite, enter the recipient email, choose Role, and tap Create invitation.
  5. The recipient must use the verified Maker email that received the invitation. They can use the secure link or manual invitation code.

After acceptance: the business becomes available to the invited account with its assigned role and inherited business Paid features, subject to the four-active-user limit.

Android release: Maker 1.0.0 is live on Google Play. If a purchase, restore, invitation link or team acceptance does not behave as expected, contact support with the account email and a description of the step that failed.

12

Additional features and troubleshooting

Recover carefully and keep evidence

Sync will not finish

Stay signed in, keep local app data, reconnect, then tap Sync now. If a conflict is reported, contact support before clearing anything.

Wrong stock balance

Use Complete count rather than rewriting history. Maker appends a variance movement so the audit trail remains intact.

Wrong team account

Use Switch account and sign in with the verified email that received the invitation. Store billing identity is not the same as Maker email identity.

Need help

Open More → Help & feedback, or email developer@cloverbeemaker.ca.

Other useful controls

Business logo adds your brand inside Maker. Dark mode changes the app theme. Account provides account export and deletion controls. Receiving history, Inventory counts, and Make history retain the operational record behind current balances.
