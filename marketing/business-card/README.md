Artwork setup:
- Finished trim: 2.5 in x 2.5 in.
- Bleed: 0.125 in on every side.
- Artwork file: 2.75 in x 2.75 in / 825 x 825 px at 300 dpi.
- Background: Warm Cream `#FFF8EC`.
- Official mark: repository-root `cloverbee-mark.png`, used exactly as supplied.
- Typography: Inter only.
- QR target: `https://cloverbeemaker.ca/card`.
- Front copy: `CloverBee Maker`; `Run the business behind what you make.`; `Built for small-batch businesses.`
- Back copy: `Inventory • Production`; `Orders • Sales`; `Scan to learn more`; `cloverbeemaker.ca/card`.

`generate_card.py` is the deterministic source. Do not recreate the logo or QR with an image-generation model.

The print-ready PNGs are archived in Google Drive under the CloverBee project marketing/business-card backup. A small initial order of roughly 50-100 cards is planned.
