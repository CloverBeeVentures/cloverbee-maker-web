## Image-generation prompt set

The four source photographs were generated with the built-in image-generation tool as square, photorealistic workshop scenes. They depict, respectively: ceramic costing materials and a scale; small-batch soap production; handmade jewelry order packing; and a craft-market sales table. Every prompt required warm cream, sage, espresso and restrained burnt-orange tones; generous upper-left copy space; natural workshop texture; and no text, logos, watermarks, navy, neon gradients, invented clovers or bees. The official CloverBee mark, typography and all copy were then added deterministically by `generate_campaign.py`.
