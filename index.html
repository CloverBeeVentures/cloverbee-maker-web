Payments should feel like part of Maker

Square integration is planned for launch.

The target is simple: start the payment from the Maker sale or order, send the exact amount to Square, complete the card payment, and return to Maker with the payment recorded once and the balance updated automatically.

Square remains the payment processor. Maker remains the source of truth for products, inventory, orders, customers and business records.

Integrated Square payment is being finalized during beta and is not represented as live in the current pre-release build.
