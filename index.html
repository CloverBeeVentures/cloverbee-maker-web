Our Story

Built because we needed it ourselves.

A couple of years ago, my wife and I started an apiary. We knew almost nothing about beekeeping, and by the time harvest season came, we realized that keeping the bees alive had been the easy part.

Selling honey, though? That got complicated. We had jars and orders and payments and deliveries. And every night my wife was untangling this long checklist just to figure out tomorrow.

We didn't need expensive software. We just needed something simple. So, I built it. The first version ran on our phones and a computer we had at home. It worked.

Then, friends and customers saw it and said some version of, “Wait, what is that?” We realized we weren't the only ones with this problem.

CloverBee Maker grew out of that moment. It's built for people who make and sell real things without turning a small business into another full-time job.

Payments should feel like part of Maker

Square payment handoff is built into Maker 1.0.

Start from a Maker sale or order, send the exact CAD balance to Square Point of Sale, complete the payment in Square, and return to Maker. A validated success is recorded once and the Maker balance updates automatically.

Square remains the payment processor. Maker remains the source of truth for products, inventory, orders, customers and business records.

Square Point of Sale must be installed and configured separately.
