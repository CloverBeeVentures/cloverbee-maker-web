September 9, 2026 Build 1 Version 1.0.0

The first Apple-distributed Maker build has been uploaded through the production signing pipeline and submitted for external TestFlight Beta App Review.

Included in this candidate

  • Products, materials, recipes, production and inventory workflows.
  • Customers, orders, sales, payments, expenses and reports.
  • Offline-first local storage with cloud synchronization when a connection is available.
  • Dark mode, account recovery, export and recoverable account deletion.
  • Free 1.0 launch configuration with no advertising or in-app purchases.

Status: Awaiting Apple Beta App Review before the first external tester round. A final public release candidate will follow physical iPhone testing and any verified fixes.
