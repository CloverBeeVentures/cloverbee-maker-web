v1.0.0

First public iOS release

iOS App Review
Build 7 Version 1.0.0 Manual release after Apple approval

CloverBee Maker 1.0 is the first public iPhone and iPad release: a complete small-business workflow for people who make and sell physical products.

Included in Maker 1.0

  • Products, variants, materials, Recipes, production and inventory.
  • Orders, reservations, customers, suppliers, sales and payment tracking.
  • Square Point of Sale handoff from Maker with exact-amount return handling.
  • Expenses, configurable taxes, costing, reporting and business-data export.
  • Offline-first operation with cloud synchronization when a connection is available.
  • Free and Maker Paid plans in the same app, with no advertising on either plan.
  • Paid receipt/source-document storage, custom expense organization, Product Families, customer/supplier analysis, labour economics, reminders and richer exports.
  • Server-verified Apple subscription purchase and restore.
  • Recoverable account deletion and production-safe data cleanup.

Status: Build 7 has been submitted to Apple App Review. The public release remains manual and will be announced only after Apple approval, the owner releases it, and the live App Store build passes a final smoke test.
