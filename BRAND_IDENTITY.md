# CloverBee Maker Brand Identity

Status: **LOCKED — 2026-09-09**

This file is the canonical visual-identity reference for CloverBee Maker website, app-store assets, social media, business cards, and future marketing creative unless Jordon explicitly revisits the brand.

## Brand direction

**Workshop software**: warm, capable, practical, maker-first.

The product should feel at home beside a scale, label printer, market tote, inventory shelf, and workbench. It should not look like generic SaaS, a fintech product, a scrapbook/craft template, or a rustic farmers-market cliché.

## Official mark

- Use the existing full-colour CloverBee clover mark exactly as supplied.
- Website asset: `cloverbee-mark.png`.
- Do not redraw, reinterpret, simplify, add leaves, add a bee, recolour, or ask generative image tools to recreate the mark.
- Preserve the original logo artwork/colouring. The palette below governs surrounding brand design; it does not authorize recolouring the logo.
- On a light background, use the full-colour mark directly.
- On a dark espresso-brown field, use the full-colour mark inside a warm-cream holding shape unless an approved inverse logo is deliberately created later.

## Locked colour palette — Palette A

| Role | Colour | HEX | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand anchor | Clover Green | `#0A6739` | Brand cues, emphasis, selected headings, restrained fields |
| Main dark neutral | Espresso Brown | `#3B2A22` | Primary text, dark backgrounds, icon strokes, strong dividers |
| Accent | Burnt Orange | `#C96A2B` | Small functional accents, highlights, rules, selected icon details |
| Main light surface | Warm Cream | `#FFF8EC` | Primary backgrounds and cards |
| Secondary light surface | Soft Sage | `#DDE9DF` | Secondary panels, subtle backgrounds, supportive UI states |

### Colour rules

- **No navy.** Navy is removed from the CloverBee Maker identity.
- Espresso Brown replaces navy wherever a dark structural colour is required.
- Burnt Orange is the warm accent. Do not layer a separate honey-gold accent on top unless the palette is deliberately reopened.
- Prefer Warm Cream over stark white for branded surfaces.
- Soft Sage supports the green mark without competing with it.
- Use colour by function, not merely to fill space.

## Typography

Use **Inter / system-compatible Inter-style sans-serif only** for the current brand system.

- Headlines: Inter ExtraBold / Bold.
- Subheads: Inter Bold / SemiBold.
- Labels and navigation: Inter SemiBold.
- Body: Inter Regular.
- Keep hierarchy strong and typography clean.
- **No script font** in the current locked system.
- Do not introduce serif display faces, handwriting fonts, or decorative type merely for marketing artwork.

The Maker app currently has no custom font declaration, so this clean sans-serif direction also avoids unnecessary divergence between product and marketing.

## Graphic language

- Rounded, friendly, practical line icons.
- Default icon/divider stroke: Espresso Brown.
- Burnt Orange may be used as a small functional accent within icons or rules.
- Clean spacing and strong hierarchy.
- Restrained rounded corners.
- Warm Cream and Soft Sage surfaces.
- Subtle oversized clover watermark/pattern is acceptable when it uses the real mark and remains secondary to content.
- Simple rules/dividers are preferred over busy diagonals or ornamental filler.

### Avoid

- Navy in any form.
- Neon/SaaS gradients.
- Generic blue technology styling.
- Rustic kraft-paper clichés.
- Random botanical sprigs or foliage not derived from the actual mark.
- Invented bee/clover logos.
- Decorative slogans used merely to fill empty space.
- Tiny low-contrast script copy.

## Brand mood

**Warm · Capable · Practical · Maker-first**

Desired impression: a serious small-business tool made specifically for people who make physical things.

The identity should bridge two ideas:

1. the warmth and physicality of a real maker/workshop business; and
2. the clarity, trust, and structure expected from business software.

## Current positioning copy

Primary positioning currently approved for marketing/card exploration:

- **Run the business behind what you make.**
- **Built for small-batch businesses.**

The website already expands the feature story beyond short card copy. Business-card feature shorthand may use the four fastest-scanning pillars:

- Inventory
- Production
- Orders
- Sales

Other capabilities such as customers, expenses, taxes, reporting, exports, and offline operation remain part of the broader product story and should be shown on the landing page/store listing rather than forcing every capability onto a small card.

## Business-card / QR guardrails

- Business-card work is paused until this identity is used as the source of truth; earlier generated card mockups are drafts, not brand authority.
- Future card artwork must be laid out deterministically using the real logo and real type, not generated as a complete image by an image model.
- Printed QR codes must be generated as real QR data, never drawn by an image generator.
- Current permanent card route: `https://cloverbeemaker.ca/card`.
- The `/card` route is intentionally CloverBee-owned so future App Store / Google Play destinations can change without reprinting cards.
